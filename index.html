The problem

Here's what actually happens when a mission ends.

Missionaries don't get their email account from Google, or from you — the mission issues it, and the mission controls it. The moment a mission officially ends, that account is disabled or handed off to the next missionary. Every weekly update. Every photo they ever attached. Every voice memo. It only ever lived in that one inbox — no export button, no warning, no grace period. It's just gone.

How it works
What exactly does Mission Bridge Archive save?

Every email your missionary sends to their Mission Bridge Archive address, along with any photos and voice memos attached directly to that email.

We can't capture Google Drive or Google Photos links — if a missionary shares a link instead of attaching a file directly, we flag it on the dashboard so your family knows something didn't come through, instead of silently losing it. Very large emails (lots of high-resolution photos at once) may also not go through, so we recommend a handful of regular photos per email.

Does my missionary need to do anything different?

No. We give you a dedicated email address to add to the same distribution list your missionary already emails home to — it works exactly like adding one more recipient. They keep sending their normal weekly update exactly like before. No app to download, no new habit to remember mid-mission.

Will this cause any issues with the mission or their mission-issued account?

Mission Bridge Archive never contacts the mission or the mission-issued account directly, and it doesn't change anything about how your missionary emails home. It simply receives a copy of the same email your missionary is already sending to their normal distribution list — the same as if you'd added one more family member's address to that list.

What if my missionary's email address changes mid-mission?

Just email us and we'll update it on your account so nothing sent from the new address gets missed.

Privacy & access
Who can see our family's dashboard?

Only people you share the private dashboard link with. There's no password to remember or reset — but that also means anyone who has the link can view it, so treat it like you would any private family link and don't post it publicly. See our Security & Privacy page for the full details.

Is this only for LDS missionaries?

Right now, yes — Mission Bridge Archive is built specifically around how LDS missions work: the email cadence, the account structure, and the timing of when accounts get wiped after a mission ends. It isn't built for other missionary or volunteer programs yet.

I don't have my missionary's call or email yet — can I still sign up?

Yes — choose "Don't have their call yet" on the signup form and we'll reserve your spot at the founding rate. Once you have their mission call and email, we'll send you a link to finish setting up in about a minute.
